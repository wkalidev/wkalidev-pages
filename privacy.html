Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This page explains what data is collected when you visit about.wkalidev.com. In short: nothing, unless you accept analytics.

1. Analytics

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits: pages viewed, how you arrived (for example from YouTube or X), approximate country, device type, and clicks on outbound links. This helps me see which pages and videos are useful. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4.

If you click No thanks or ignore the banner, Google Analytics runs in consent mode with analytics storage denied: no analytics cookies are set.

2. Your choice

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (key wk-consent). To change it, clear this site's data in your browser settings and reload the page: the banner will appear again.

3. Contact and forms

There are no forms on this site. If you email hello@wkalidev.com, your email is used only to reply to you.

4. Third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel and uses Google Fonts. Analytics data, if you accept, is processed by Google. Links to YouTube, X, Ko-fi and other platforms follow those platforms' own privacy policies.

5. Contact

Questions or data requests: hello@wkalidev.com
